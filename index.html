Features

Everything your
media deserves.

🔍

Auto-scan & organize

Point it at a folder and Localflix automatically finds, names, and organizes your movies and shows — no manual tagging.

🎨

Poster art & metadata

Fetches artwork, descriptions, and episode info from AniList and TMDB so your library looks as good as it feels.

▶️

Opens in your player

Plays files in VLC, MPV, or whatever you have installed. No codec wars, no re-encoding, just press play.

📈

Watch progress tracking

Keeps track of where you left off across episodes and movies. Your Continue Watching row is always up to date.

🌐

Works fully offline

No account, no subscription, no cloud. Your files stay on your machine. Internet only needed for fetching posters.

⚡

Single .exe, no install

Download one file, double-click, done. No Python, no Node.js, no dependencies. It just works.
